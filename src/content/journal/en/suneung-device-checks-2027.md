Ahead of the **2027 admissions Suneung, held in 2026**, concerns about smart glasses and miniature cameras have brought electronic-device checks into focus. Candidates need clear information about what they may bring. Exam teams need a consistent procedure for checking and recording concerns.

This guide reflects **official announcements checked on 17 November 2026**. For an actual exam, follow the latest instructions from the Ministry of Education, local education authority and exam organizer. The linked official sources are in Korean.

## Electronic devices candidates must not bring

The Ministry of Education lists phones, smartwatches, smart glasses and Bluetooth earphones among prohibited items. A candidate who unavoidably brings a prohibited item must hand it to a proctor before the first session. Leaving it unused does not make possession permissible. Consult the [ministry’s exam misconduct guidance](https://www.moe.go.kr/sub/infoRenew.do?m=011301&page=011301&s=moe) for the full list and rules.

When packing, check inner pockets and the clothes you normally wear. Ask the organizer in advance if you are unsure about the functions of your glasses or watch, or need a device for a health-related reason.

## Why smart glasses are receiving attention

In its 17 November statement, the government announced stronger supervision, including checks of glasses, in response to cheating involving electronic devices. See the [official statement on Suneung cheating prevention](https://www.korea.kr/briefing/pressReleaseView.do?newsId=156789621).

Smart glasses can resemble ordinary eyewear. Exam staff therefore need to apply the prescribed checks consistently. An online product photo or a familiar device name is not enough to establish that a candidate has cheated.

## Preparation notes for exam teams

These are suggestions for an institution’s own planning, not a substitute for official Suneung proctoring instructions.

- **Source of instructions:** Share the version and date of the rules in use.
- **Responsibility:** Agree who receives a device-related concern and who makes the decision.
- **Approved equipment:** Check how operational equipment and previously approved devices will be handled.
- **Records:** Agree how to record the time, direct observations, action taken and responsible staff member.

Make unresolved questions explicit. “New wireless signal received; device not located” is a different finding from “candidate observed using a device.” Clear records help the next reviewer understand what was actually established.

## Where does a wireless detection app fit?

Smart Detector is an Android supporting tool for checking wireless signals a phone can receive and recording on-site findings. A received signal does not establish that a device is a pair of smart glasses, identify its owner or prove misconduct.

The absence of a signal does not establish that no device is present. Results depend on what the device transmits and what the phone and operating system can observe. Read [how to interpret signals from smart glasses and earphones](/en/journal/what-the-app-can-observe/) for more detail.

**Use an app or other equipment during an actual exam only under procedures authorized by the organizer.** This guide does not indicate that Smart Detector has been designated or approved as official Suneung equipment.

## Trying the workflow in a school or practice exam

Where the organizer permits it, try measuring an empty room first. Then observe what changes as people and devices enter. Comparing received signals with devices you can confirm during practice helps staff learn how to read the screen.
