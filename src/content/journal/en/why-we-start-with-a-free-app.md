Our first product is an Android supporting app that checks wireless signals a phone can receive. It records empty-room signals as a baseline before the exam, flags new signals during the session and lets proctors log what they checked.

The app is free and requires no account. Teachers and proctors should be able to try it without cost barriers and judge for themselves whether it fits their exams.

## Why field feedback matters

Screens that look clear on a developer’s desk can feel less obvious while supervising a live exam. Real rooms show whether target signals are easy to pick out and whether any crucial detail is missing from the report.

Tell us what felt confusing or what features you needed. Voluntary feedback shapes both app updates and our upcoming dedicated hardware. Signal and check records stay on your phone—we do not upload or collect them automatically.

Signals in the app are leads for a proctor to check. A signal never proves cheating, and no signal never proves a room is clear. We state these limits plainly so staff keep final checks and decisions in human hands under their institution’s rules.

## What comes after the free app

Next, we are preparing two products for schools and exam organizers: a dedicated device that logs wireless signals steadily throughout an exam, and a platform that assesses how students work and revise with AI. Both are in concept and research preparation, and we will share validated specs and timelines once testing is complete.

For now, [explore the free app](/en/app/) and test it before your next exam (the app interface is currently in Korean). Your feedback helps us build more practical tools.
