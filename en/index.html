Check the signals
before the exam.

In an empty room, select “Room is empty” (방이 비어 있습니다) and listen for 90 seconds to establish a baseline. Once everyone is seated, review the remaining signals and select “Start exam” (시험 시작).

If people are already present, select “People are already here” (이미 사람이 있습니다).

Notice new signals
at a glance.

A large count and a list show signals first received after the exam starts. When an alert arrives, select the signal and check what is happening in the room.

Keep the app visible during the exam.

Watch signal strength
as you check the room.

See how the selected signal’s received strength changes as you move. Check the room and record your assessment.

Signal strength does not indicate distance or direction.

Smart Detector helps you decide what to check in person.
Wireless signals alone do not establish cheating.

What does it observe?
Wireless signals your phone can receive, including BLE advertisements, discoverable Bluetooth devices, and Wi-Fi access points.
What can it tell you?
You can review received signals and changes over time. The app cannot determine an owner, a seat, whether a device is recording, or whether cheating has occurred.
What does a count of zero mean?
No new signals have been received. It does not guarantee that no devices are present. Devices that are not transmitting may go unobserved.

Before
you begin.

Can it find smart glasses or every electronic device?

No. A device must transmit a signal that your phone can receive. Detection is not guaranteed for devices that stop advertising after connecting, are powered off, or use only cellular connectivity. Detection performance for individual smart glasses models also needs separate testing.

Does it work on iPhone?

Currently, only Android 12 or later is supported. An iPhone app is not available. The signals your phone can receive and its permission settings may vary by manufacturer and operating system.

How do I install the APK?

Download the APK using this page, then open the file on your Android phone. Verify the source and follow the installation instructions shown on your device. Device security settings or an institution’s management policies may restrict installation.

Does it need internet access? Where are records stored?

After downloading the app, you can use it on its own without an internet connection. When online, it sends only app usage statistics (Firebase Analytics); it never sends received signals or exam records, and it does not collect an advertising ID. Wireless observations are stored on your device. You can save or share the report after an exam and delete records from the app’s headquarters menu.

Can I turn off the screen during an exam?

Keep the app open and visible. Reception stops when you leave the app screen. If you return during an exam, select “Resume reception” (다시 수신) and check the reception status. Periods without reception are recorded in the report.
